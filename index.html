How the voice works
There is no emotion dial on this model — so there is no fake one in this UI. Tone comes from three real things: which speaker you pick (each has a described character), delivery (how much variation the model introduces), and the writing itself — punctuation, sentence length and rhythm outweigh any slider.

Phonon-31m Apache 2.0 WebAssembly No server Your text never leaves the device. The model downloads once, then it works offline.

One-time download

The speech model is about 65 MB. It downloads once and is then cached by your browser, so later visits start instantly and work without a connection.
